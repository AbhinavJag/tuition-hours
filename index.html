How it works

Saved scenarios and share links live in your browser. There are no accounts and nothing is uploaded.

Prices before today are estimates. Average public four-year tuition and fees rose 221% from 2000 to 2024, so your price grows smoothly from today's price divided by 3.21.

Minimum wage is the federal rate: $5.15 through 2006, $5.85 in 2007, $6.55 in 2008, $7.25 since 2009.

No taxes, books, housing or loans.
